 ## HerMarket – Women Entrepreneur E-Commerce Platform



HerMarket is a web-based e-commerce platform designed to support women entrepreneurs by providing a dedicated marketplace to showcase and sell their products online.

The platform allows sellers to manage products and orders while customers can browse products, add items to cart, place orders, manage addresses, maintain wishlists, and leave reviews.

---

##  Key Implementations

### Inventory Management

* Seller sets product stock.
* Stock automatically decreases after successful orders.
* Customers cannot purchase out-of-stock products.

### Review System

* Customers can review only delivered products.
* Duplicate reviews are prevented.
* Average ratings are displayed on product pages.

### Wishlist System

* Save products for future purchase.
* Remove products from wishlist.
* Move products to cart.
